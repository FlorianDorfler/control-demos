Control demos

Interactive companions to Control Systems 227-0103-00, ETH Zürich.

Lecture 7 · optimal control

LQR tuner for the planar drone

Move any diagonal entry of Q or R and the gain is re-solved from the Riccati equation, so the drone you watch is always flying the optimal controller for the cost you just wrote down.
Lecture 8 · optimal estimation

Kalman tuner for the car with two sensors

Change what the filter believes about Qw and Rv while the car, the road, and the readings stay one fixed realization, and watch the gap open between the accuracy the filter promises itself and the accuracy it delivers.
Lecture 10 · feedback & PID control

PID tuner for the autonomous car

Move KP, KI, and KD and watch the step response, the recovery from a disturbance, and the noise reaching the actuator redraw together, with stability read off the filtered loop that actually gets implemented.
Lecture 11 · closed-loop stability

Margin bench for the car over a network

Raise the gain and the loop gets faster while the delay it can survive gets shorter. The Nyquist plot, the Bode plot, and the step response move together, and the gain margin stays infinite until the first millisecond of delay arrives.
Lecture 12 · loop shaping design

Loop shaper for the car of Lecture 10

Shape the same loop three ways, a lead compensator tuned forward, an inversion design, and internal model control, against the specification drawn behind the curves. A switch puts a zero at s = +2 into the plant, and all three run into one wall.
Lecture 13 · augmented architectures

Pairing bench for a two-by-two plant

Two decentralized loops, each designed as if the other did not exist, on a plant whose coupling you control. Watch the closed-loop poles cross over at the frequency where the relative gain array changes sign, and the transmission zero walk in behind them.
Lecture 14 · digital control

Aliasing bench for the sensor and the sampler

Sweep a disturbance past the Nyquist frequency and watch it fold back down into the loop's own bandwidth, sampled directly and through an anti-alias filter, with the folding drawn on a frequency strip underneath.
Lecture 14 · digital control

Sampling and emulation bench for the cruise control

Drag the sampling period, pick how the controller was turned into code, and watch the discrete poles walk out of the unit circle at a boundary that moves by a factor of nearly three depending only on which substitution you used.
Each demo is a single self-contained HTML file. It runs offline in the browser, with nothing to install.
