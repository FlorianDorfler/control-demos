Argument principle bench for a rational function

Function Π(s) = ∏(s − zi) / ∏(s − pj), with the zeros and the poles placed by dragging them in the left panel  ·  Contour the standard D-contour, up the imaginary axis from −iR to +iR and back along a clockwise arc of radius R, so D is traversed clockwise and encloses the right half plane  ·  Reading a point travels around D while its image traces Π(D). The argument principle says that image encircles the origin Z − P times clockwise.
