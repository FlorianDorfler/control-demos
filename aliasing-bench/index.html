Why the corner sits below Nyquist

A first-order filter is down only 3 dB at its own corner, so a corner placed at the Nyquist frequency still lets most of the disturbance through to the sampler, and whatever arrives above Nyquist comes back folded to a frequency the controller will act on. The script puts the corner a factor of four below.
