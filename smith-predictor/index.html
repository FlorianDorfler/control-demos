Notes. The crossover ωc and the phase margin are read off L(s) = K(s)G(s), the loop the controller was designed against. A transport delay does not change L's magnitude, so it moves no crossover; it subtracts λωc radians of phase there, and the delay margin is the phase margin of the delay-free design divided by ωc. With the default PI the zero cancels the plant pole, L = 1/s, the crossover is at 1 rad/s and the delay margin is exactly π/2 ≈ 1.57 s. Overshoot and settling time (2 %) are measured on the predictor's response. With an exact model the predictor's closed loop is T(s)e−λs: the delay-free design, delayed. The delay is not removed, it is moved out of the loop and onto the output, which is the one thing this page is here to show.
